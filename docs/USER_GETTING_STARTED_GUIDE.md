# FocusFlow: Getting Started & Goal Optimization Guide

Welcome to **FocusFlow**! This guide is designed to help you get familiar with the system, optimize your study workflow, and align your daily habits to achieve your academic and personal goals without feeling burnt out.

Unlike traditional planners, FocusFlow doesn't punish you for missing sessions—it adapts, keeps you honest with browser analytics, and matches you with friends for mutual motivation.

---

## 🚀 The FocusFlow Philosophy
1. **Realistic, Not Rigid**: Schedulers fail because life happens. FocusFlow adapts your schedule dynamically when plans change.
2. **Behavioral Alignment**: Planning is only 10% of the battle. The companion Chrome Extension helps bridge the gap by enforcing your plan at the browser level.
3. **Sustainable Performance**: Burnout is the enemy of productivity. Pre-defining sleep, commutes, and rest breaks ensures you work sustainably.

---

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Workflow for Goal Success

### Step 1: Define Your Baseline Rules (Onboarding & Settings)
When you first register, or inside the **Settings > Profile & Lifestyle** tab:
* **Classes & Lectures**: Input your fixed class times. FocusFlow locks these slots first.
* **Protected Blocks (Lifestyle)**: Declare recurring commitments you value (e.g. Gym 18:00 - 19:00, Gaming, or Family Dinner). The scheduler treats these as *untouchable*, scheduling study sessions around them.
* **Study Preferences**: Declare your target study hours and preferred session length (e.g., 25-minute Pomodoros vs 50-minute blocks).

### Step 2: Organize Your Tasks with Constraints
Go to the **Tasks** page and click **+ Create Task**. When adding academic goals, define their parameters carefully:
* **Priority**:
  * *Critical*: Crucial homework/prep. If missed, it *must* be rescheduled the same day.
  * *High / Medium / Low*: Standard items that can shift if needed.
* **Flexibility**:
  * *Fixed*: Can only be done at a specific time (e.g., a group study meeting).
  * *Flexible*: Can be moved around by the scheduler's rearrangement engine.
* **Skip Cost**: Tells the scheduler the consequence of missing it. High skip-cost tasks will trigger louder recommendations to study.
* **Share with Pod**: Toggle on to share progress updates with your accountability circle. Toggle off for private tasks.

### Step 3: Run the Deep Work Loop (Timer & Extension)
To log focus sessions and get tasks done:
1. Ensure the **FocusFlow Companion Extension** is loaded in your browser and synced with your account token.
2. Open the extension popup or the web **Focus Session** page.
3. Select the specific task you are working on from the dropdown.
4. Go to **⚙ Configure Settings** inside the extension popup and input:
   * Your target focus, short break, and long break times.
   * Your **Distraction Blocklist** (e.g., `instagram.com, youtube.com`) and your **Useful Whitelist** (e.g., `google.com, github.com`) to whitelist sites you need for research.
5. Click **Start Focus**. Distracting sites will instantly be blocked, and any attempt to visit them will redirect to a focus shield.
6. When the timer finishes, rate the difficulty and review your distractions. You earn **10 XP** for every completed session!

### Step 4: Handle Missed Sessions Without Guilt (Adaptive Recovery)
If you miss a scheduled study block (e.g., you overslept or got distracted):
1. Upon returning to the Dashboard, a **Missed Session Modal** will gently appear.
2. **Be Honest**:
   * If the task is urgent, select **Critical**. FocusFlow's rearrangement engine will immediately search your remaining day, preserve your sleep and gym blocks, and slot the study task back into a free hour.
   * If it can wait, select **Important** (postpones it to tomorrow) or **Optional** (skips it without penalty).
3. Review your newly adjusted schedule on the **Schedule** page.

### Step 5: Leverage Your Social Pod (Accountability)
Under the **Social Pods** tab:
* **Join or Form a Pod**: Invite 2 or 3 classmates or friends.
* **The Daily Streak**: Keep the group streak alive! Work together to make sure every single member logs at least one focus session every calendar day.
* **Quests**: Set up collaborative group quests (e.g. "Prepare for Midterm - 200 XP target"). Every member's completed focus sessions add XP to the progress bar.
* **Nudges**: Use quick emoji nudges on the leaderboard:
  * Poke (👉) a friend who hasn't studied yet.
  * Clap (👏) for a friend who just finished a study block.
  * Encourage (💪) someone working on a difficult task.

### Step 6: Review Analytics & ML Recommendations
Every weekend, visit the **Analytics** page:
* **Burnout Risk**: If the risk bar is red, consider adding more protected blocks or lowering your target study hours.
* **Focus Peak Hours**: Review the optimal times the ML service recommends for your study sessions. Adjust your onboarding preferences to match this peak time.
* **AI Coach Cards**: Read the weekly tips. If the model alerts you that your distraction rates are high, update your extension blocklist.
